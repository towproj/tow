# Governance

## Roles
- **Contributors:** anyone who opens an issue, reviews a pull request, or sends a change.
- **Maintainers** ([MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)): review and merge pull requests, cut releases, triage issues and security reports, and decide the project's direction.

tow starts with a single maintainer. As the project grows, the goal is maintainers from more than one organization.

## Decisions
Decisions are made in the open, in issues and pull requests, by lazy consensus: a proposal goes ahead when no maintainer objects within a reasonable time (a week for a design, less for a fix). When maintainers disagree, they vote, by simple majority of the maintainers. Changes to this document need two thirds.

## Becoming a maintainer
A contributor with a record of substantial, quality contributions and reviews over several months can be nominated by a maintainer, or ask to be. The maintainers vote; once accepted, the new maintainer is added to MAINTAINERS.md in a pull request.

A maintainer who is inactive for six months, or who asks to step down, becomes an emeritus maintainer.

## Code of Conduct
Everyone in the project follows the [CNCF Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
